# Products used across the wearable ecosystem

<p class="page-intro">My product work spans firmware-facing features, communication protocols, data processing, and user experience across mobile, desktop, RTOS, and WearOS platforms.</p>

<div class="project-grid">
  <article class="project-card">
    <span class="project-card__number">01 · Android</span>
    <h2>Galaxy Wearable</h2>
    <p>The companion app for Samsung smart rings, fitness bands, and smartwatches—supporting firmware updates, notifications, synchronization, and device settings through dedicated plugins.</p>
    <ul class="tag-list"><li>FOTA</li><li>BLE</li><li>Protocols</li><li>LBS</li><li>Diagnostics</li></ul>
    <p>I developed and optimized FOTA, location-based services, notifications, log-dump functionality, and secure device-to-app communication.</p>
    <a class="project-card__link" href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.samsung.android.app.watchmanager&hl=en">View on Google Play →</a>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <span class="project-card__number">02 · Health platform</span>
    <h2>Samsung Health</h2>
    <p>A health and fitness platform that integrates with Samsung wearables to collect activity, heart-rate, sleep, and workout data.</p>
    <ul class="tag-list"><li>SDK</li><li>TLV data</li><li>Synchronization</li><li>Wearables</li></ul>
    <p>I contributed to an SDK that processes TLV data and communicates between wearable applications and Samsung Health, including data conversion and real-time synchronization.</p>
    <a class="project-card__link" href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sec.android.app.shealth&hl=en">View on Google Play →</a>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <span class="project-card__number">03 · Windows</span>
    <h2>Galaxy Buds Manager</h2>
    <p>A Windows companion application for managing Samsung earbuds and keeping their firmware current.</p>
    <ul class="tag-list"><li>UWP</li><li>FOTA</li><li>Device communication</li></ul>
    <p>I contributed to the secure and reliable firmware update flow, including communication between the desktop application and earbuds.</p>
    <a class="project-card__link" href="https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9nhtlwtkfznb?hl=en-US&gl=US">View in Microsoft Store →</a>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <span class="project-card__number">04 · iOS</span>
    <h2>Samsung Galaxy Watch</h2>
    <p>An iOS companion application connecting Galaxy wearables with iPhone.</p>
    <ul class="tag-list"><li>Swift</li><li>FOTA</li><li>Protocols</li><li>Data sync</li></ul>
    <p>I worked on firmware updates, device communication protocols, synchronization, and the companion experience for managing wearables on iOS.</p>
    <a class="project-card__link" href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/samsung-galaxy-watch-gear-s/id1117310635">View in App Store →</a>
  </article>
</div>
