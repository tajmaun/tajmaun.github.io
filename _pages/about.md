## Research and innovation

My research journey began with an undergraduate thesis in computer vision and image processing. At Samsung Research, that curiosity developed into patent and disclosure work for wearable systems. As lead inventor of a WearOS boot-time optimization technique, I introduced parallel pre-loading that improved startup performance by **15%**.

I am particularly interested in human-computer interaction, high-performance software, distributed systems, cloud computing, and research that moves from a strong technical idea to measurable real-world impact.
