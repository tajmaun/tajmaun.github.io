I am a Lead Software Engineer in the Wearable Solution Lab at Samsung Research. For more than seven years I have built software for Samsung's smartwatches (Wear OS, Tizen), smart rings, fitness bands and earbuds (RTOS), and their companion apps on Android, iOS and Windows.

My engineering work keeps returning to one question: how much effort does a small device ask of its user? I have worked on getting a watch usable sooner after boot, keeping settings and restored state consistent across devices, updating firmware reliably, and deciding what a tiny screen should show. I am now turning these product problems into research questions. I am the lead inventor on a patent application for Wear OS boot-time optimization and the author of a manuscript in preparation on adaptive app selection for wearables, and I want to pursue doctoral research that combines systems engineering with human-centered evaluation.

I hold a B.Sc. (Engg.) in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Chittagong, where my thesis was on computer vision. I also have a strong background in competitive programming (HackerRank top 0.75%, Codeforces Candidate Master, rating 1930).

Research

Manuscript in preparation

Adaptive Featured App Selection through Recency-Gated Historical Usage

A wearable launcher can show only a handful of apps, and users' habits change. Ranking apps by long-term usage reacts slowly. Ranking by recent usage makes the screen churn. This work proposes a recency-gated ranking in which recent activity controls how much older history counts, combined with persistence guards and replacement penalties to keep the selection stable.

Evaluated against fixed-window and exponential-decay baselines on adaptation time, launch coverage, replacement frequency and ranking stability, using the public LSApp app-usage dataset.

Patent application · lead inventor

Improving Boot Time for Wear OS Devices

Smartwatch startup is delayed by initialization ordering, encrypted-storage dependencies and main-thread contention. I led the design of a parallel pre-loading technique that classifies start-up tasks by when their data becomes available and schedules them earlier in the boot sequence. This improved start-up performance by about 15%.

Rated A1 (high impact) in Samsung's internal IP assessment. A patent application has been filed.

Undergraduate thesis · 2017

Bengali Number Plate Detection Using Edge-Based and Contour-Based Methods

Most number-plate detection methods target Latin-script plates. This thesis presents a method for Bengali number plates in images taken under natural conditions. It localizes the plate with Sobel edge detection, Otsu's thresholding, morphological closing and contour properties, then segments characters with a contour-based approach and recognizes them with a Multi-Layer Perceptron. It reached 87% plate-detection accuracy and 70.12% character-segmentation accuracy.
